### One-liner

> A Robinhood Chain launchpad where every qualified trade can contribute to a verifiable reward economy.

### Elevator pitch (30 seconds)

LOOTING is a launchpad on Robinhood Chain. Creators launch through Pons V2 rails. Traders can buy anywhere on-chain — LOOTING, GMGN, Axiom, Trojan, or a DEX — and still earn Season XP and a Lucky Box. The box opens after you exit. Better weekly tier, better odds. Rewards can be LOOTING, tokenized stocks, or RWAs. Creators signal skin in the game with Dev Lock and public staking vaults. Pons runs the curve. LOOTING owns discovery, loot, and the season.

### What we are

- Robinhood Chain fair-launch pad with a **trade-to-loot** layer
- Wallet-native seasons (no username account)
- Verifiable eligibility: buy → XP + box → exit → open
- Trust tools: Dev Lock, permissionless staking vaults
- Built on public Pons V2 infrastructure (not a fork)

### What we are NOT

- Not a Pons fork or Pons replacement
- Not a wallet or CEX
- Not a guarantee of token returns
- Not a system designed to manufacture fake volume
- Not a hold-to-farm stock dividend pad
- Not a casino / lottery brand

## 3. Narrative

### 3.1 Story spine

1. **World** — Launchpads own the curve. Traders live on terminals (GMGN, Axiom, Trojan). Loyalty evaporates the second they leave the pad UI.  
2. **Conflict** — Rewards either trap you in one website, farm fake volume, or drip stock for holding forever. Real trades don’t count. Exits don’t count. Trust is a tweet.  
3. **Resolution** — LOOTING indexes qualifying buys **anywhere**, awards XP + a Lucky Box, unlocks loot **after exit**, and gives creators Dev Lock + public staking so skin in the game is visible on-chain.

### 3.2 Tweet-length story

> Most pads reward you for staying on their site. LOOTING rewards the trade loop — buy anywhere, exit, open loot. Season XP. Lucky Boxes. Stocks and RWAs. Dev Lock if the creator means it.

2. Problem — traders leave; rewards don’t follow; hold-farming invents fake conviction  
3. Insight — reward the **completed loop**, not the screenshot  
4. Product — trade anywhere → XP → box → open after exit  
5. Trust — Dev Lock + public vaults  
6. Chain — why Robinhood Chain (stock quotes + stock prizes)  
7. CTA — `lootingpad.com` · Season is live

#### Logo usage — STRICT (never break)

1. **Need name + mark together** → composite from **`logo-wordmark.png` only**. Pass it as a reference image every gen. Do not invent a new wordmark typeface or rearrange the icon.  
2. **Need icon only** → **`logo.png` only**. Nine glossy lime rounded squares (1 center + 8 around).  
3. **Never** invent alternate logos (cloud-smile, hollow ring, different tile counts, flat app icons, etc.).  
4. **Never** place a separate giant “LOOTING” text beside a separately redrawn icon when the wordmark file already exists — use the wordmark file.  
5. **Scale in banners:** wordmark / logo height ≈ **8–12% of banner height max**; generous padding from edges. Prefer airy layout over oversized brand block.  
6. Mascot visor happy-arcs = **character face only**, not a logo substitute.  
7. **Preferred production method for text banners:** generate mascot art without inventing a logo, then **composite** the real `logo.png` / `logo-wordmark.png` in layout (see `assets/brand/_compose_banner.py`). Do not rely on the image model to redraw the mark.  

If a generated image shows the wrong mark, reject and regenerate or composite the real PNG.

- No guarantee of token returns or Lucky Box value

- Do not celebrate or incentivize wash trading
